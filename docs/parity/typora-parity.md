### Why nothing is REA-"verified" (and what would change that)

- **Original authority.** The ledger requires the original behaviour to be characterized by `shipped-artifact`/observed (static) or runtime Evidence. Under clean-room rules the only allowed inputs are prose, so every review record is `analyst-inference`/`inferred`; every obligation keeps the `static-only` diagnostic. Closing it needs a dirty-room observation exported as Evidence and handed over as an opaque record.
- **Proof predicate.** Fixture/verifier proof must be a `rea.reconstruction-proof` record from `verify_reconstruction_obligations`; REA 6.5.0 exposes no public tool that emits it. Our verifier runs are genuine `rea.process-capture` records, so bindings stay `implemented` with `weak-fixture-authority`/`weak-verifier-authority`.
- **Auto-derived candidates.** The 5 `unknown` ledger obligations are generated by REA from the verifier process captures themselves ("Captured packaged-process lifecycle"): the ledger cannot tell a reconstruction's test run from an observation of the original, so it opens original-behaviour candidates for them. They keep the ledger status at `unknown` and are not product surfaces.
- **Rust suite.** `cargo test` (src-tauri) ran 94 tests, 2 failed (`watch_live_tests::moved_in_and_moved_out`, `watch_live_tests::own_saves_in_watched_folder` on macOS); no surface uses it as its verifier.
- **Coverage evaluator** accepts controlled-replay captures, so owned surfaces with a named passing test qualify there; package proofs lack `clean-install`.
